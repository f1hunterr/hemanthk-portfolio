# Brand kit

Shareable material that is not part of the website build (nothing here is deployed).

- `linkedin-cover/` - LinkedIn banner (light/dark, 1x/2x) and its source `banner.html`; `other-styles/` holds the alternative designs.
- `whatsapp-kit/` - poster with QR to the contact card, and ready-to-send messages.

To re-render a banner, open its `banner.html` in a browser and screenshot at the page size.
